Privacy Policy

Anime System (Discord bot) · Effective October 13, 2026

Anime System is a private Discord bot. It lets members search a catalog of anime titles kept by the server's staff and open the link saved for each title, and it lets members contact staff through support tickets. This policy explains what data the bot handles and why.

Data we handle

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs. They are used to link a ticket to its owner, to record which staff member added or edited a catalog entry, and to check staff permissions.
  • Catalog entries: the title, description, image link, watch link, and alternative names of each entry, the ID of the staff member who added or last edited it, the dates, and a counter of how many times the title was searched. Individual searches by members are not recorded and search terms are not stored.
  • Staff activity records: for staff who add, edit, or delete entries, the titles they changed and the time of their last action, and counters of tickets each staff member claimed or closed.
  • Support tickets: when you open a ticket, the bot stores your user ID, the time, the reason you typed, and the staff member who claimed it. This record is removed from the bot's files when the ticket is closed. The bot creates a private ticket channel named after your Discord username. When the ticket is closed, an HTML transcript of it is posted to a private staff channel inside Discord, and the ticket channel is deleted. Staff can also use a button to send the ticket owner a direct message asking them to return to the ticket.
  • Statistics: overall counters such as total searches, additions, edits, deletions, and tickets. These are not tied to individual members.
  • Activity logs: the bot posts logs to private staff channels inside Discord when an entry is added, edited, or deleted (with the staff member who did it), and for ticket events (opened with its reason, claimed, and closed). These logs stay on Discord.
  • Message content: the bot reads messages only to detect commands that start with the server's command prefix. Message content is processed in memory and is not stored.
  • Backups: once a day, the bot posts copies of its catalog, settings, and statistics files to a private staff channel inside Discord. These copies contain only the data described above, such as staff IDs, and do not include ticket records.

How we use data

We use this data only to run the catalog and the support system, to keep staff actions accountable, and to produce internal statistics. We do not sell data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

AI and machine learning

We do not use message content or any other user data to train machine learning or AI models.

Storage and retention

Records are stored in files on the bot's private server and are accessible only to the bot owner. Backups and logs are kept in private channels on Discord. We keep data for as long as it is needed to operate the bot, or until deletion is requested. Open-ticket records are deleted when the ticket is closed.

Links to other websites

The catalog contains links to watch pages and images hosted by other parties. Opening a link takes you to a site we do not control, and that site's own privacy policy applies there.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to delete the data linked to your Discord user ID, including ticket records and any mention of you in the catalog or staff records. Contact us using the details below and we will act on the request within a reasonable time. Copies already posted in Discord channels are controlled by the server's staff and can be deleted on request.

Children

The bot is intended for Discord users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
